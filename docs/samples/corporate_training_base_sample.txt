Training Loop Corporate Training Base Sample

Company overview
Training Loop helps companies train employees with AI role-play scenarios. The platform turns company policies, customer segments, and communication rules into short practice tasks. Managers use the admin dashboard to upload materials, create a knowledge base, generate assignments, and review employee results.

Core communication rules
1. Be specific and connect every claim to approved company material.
2. Do not promise business results, refunds, discounts, integrations, or delivery dates without confirmed data.
3. Explain AI limitations clearly: AI can help practice and draft responses, but a human manager owns final decisions.
4. Always name the next step: schedule a demo, collect requirements, escalate to a manager, or send the correct policy link.
5. Stay calm with frustrated clients. Acknowledge the concern before explaining the rule.
6. Protect confidential information. Never reveal another client profile, internal pricing logic, or private employee scores.

Client types
- B2B enterprise client: cares about security, reporting, integrations, and measurable team adoption.
- VIP client: expects fast response, personal attention, and a clear owner for the next step.
- New client: needs simple explanations, examples, and reassurance about onboarding.
- Difficult client: may challenge the value, demand exceptions, or ask for guarantees the company cannot give.

AI product limitations
- AI-generated training scenarios are drafts and can contain mistakes.
- AI feedback should be reviewed against company policy before being used for formal performance decisions.
- AI cannot guarantee employee improvement, sales growth, or customer satisfaction by itself.
- Uploaded materials should not include secrets, passwords, payment data, or unnecessary personal data.

Good response examples
Example 1:
Client: "Can you promise our team will improve by 30% in one month?"
Employee: "I cannot promise a fixed result without your baseline data. What we can do is run a pilot, measure completion and quality scores, and review the progress after the first training cycle."

Example 2:
Client: "Your competitor is cheaper. Why should we pay more?"
Employee: "Let's compare by your goals, not only price. You mentioned manager visibility and consistent practice. Training Loop includes scenario generation, assignment tracking, and analytics in one workflow."

Example 3:
Client: "Give me another customer's results."
Employee: "I cannot share private client results. I can show anonymized examples and explain what metrics are normally tracked."

Poor response examples
Poor: "Yes, our AI will definitely improve everyone."
Better: "The AI helps employees practice consistently, but results depend on content quality, manager follow-up, and participation."

Poor: "That is not our problem."
Better: "I understand why that is frustrating. Let me check the policy and suggest the safest next step."

Scoring criteria
- Accuracy: answer matches uploaded materials and company rules.
- Confidence: employee explains clearly without overpromising.
- Objection handling: employee acknowledges concern and addresses the real business need.
- Policy compliance: employee respects privacy, AI limitations, and escalation rules.
- Next step: employee ends with a concrete action.
