1 Philosophy

Warm Outlines

Instead of stark, aggressive black (#000), it uses dark chocolate (#2B1E16) for outlines. This keeps layouts high-contrast yet friendly and soft.

Flat Plush Shadows

Components resemble physical tokens. They have flat, solid offsets (3px to 6px) that give a chunky, tactile appearance, resembling a playful desktop board game.

Active Squish

Buttons and active elements physicalize interactions. When clicked, they sink down dynamically along the Y-axis, reducing the shadow and creating a squishy click feel.
